Privacy policy

pennyWise · Last updated: [DATE]

pennyWise is a free app that helps you track your money. It is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This page explains what data the app keeps, why, where, and how you can delete it. If anything is unclear, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • We keep only what the app needs to work and to sync between your devices.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • You can export your data and delete your account at any time, in the app.

What we keep

DataWhy
Email address, and a name if you give oneTo create your account and let you sign in.
Password, stored only as a one-way hash (argon2id)To check your password. We cannot read it.
Your Google account ID, if you sign in with GoogleTo recognise you when you sign in with Google.
Main currency, language and time zoneTo show totals in your currency and put each expense in the right day and month.
What you enter: accounts, transactions, categories, notes, budgets, goals and recurring billsThis is the app. It is synced so it is safe if you lose your phone and so it appears on your other devices.
Receipt photos you attachTo show them with the transaction. Stored in a private bucket; only your signed-in app gets short-lived links to them.
Device name and a push token (only if notifications are on)To list your signed-in devices and to send budget and bill reminders.
IP address, device type and time of sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password resets, exports and currency changesTo protect your account and stop abuse.
Server request logs (time, address called, IP address)To keep the service running and find errors.

We do not collect your contacts, location, SMS messages or bank logins. The app does not read your bank account.

Where it is stored

On your phone, in an encrypted database. On our server at [HOSTING PROVIDER] in [COUNTRY]. Receipt photos, export files and database backups are stored in Cloudflare R2, a private storage service. All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS.

Who else is involved

  • Google: only if you choose "Sign in with Google", and for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) when they are turned on.
  • Cloudflare: stores receipt photos, exports and backups for us.
  • [HOSTING PROVIDER]: runs our server.
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank: our server downloads public exchange rates. None of your data is sent.

These companies process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Export files: deleted 7 days after they are made.
  • Security events: 180 days. Server request logs: about two weeks.
  • Backups: copies of the database are kept for up to 6 months, then deleted. After you delete your account, your data disappears from backups as they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • See and take your data: export it as CSV or PDF in the app.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all your data from the app and our server at once; files are removed within an hour. If you cannot use the app, see Delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK), you may have more rights, such as to object or to complain to a data protection authority. Write to us and we will help.

Children

pennyWise is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly keep their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
