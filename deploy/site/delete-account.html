Delete your pennyWise account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open pennyWise and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, type your password (not needed for Google sign-in) and confirm.

Your account closes at once and you are signed out on every device.

Without the app

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my pennyWise account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything: your profile, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring bills, receipt photos, export files and sign-in sessions. This cannot be undone. Want a copy first? Export your data from the app before deleting.

Security records of your account are deleted with it. Database backups expire within 6 months.

See also our privacy policy.
